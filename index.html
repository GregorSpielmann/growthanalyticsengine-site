The Gap

Most AI investments fail because the data foundation isn't ready

98% of organizations report urgency to deploy AI. Only 13% say they're fully ready. The gap isn't in the models — it's in structured data, governed definitions, and trusted analytics context.

The Analytics Maturity Self-Assessment helps you understand exactly where you are — and what to fix first.

Start the Assessment →

Stage 1 — Reactive

Ad-hoc reporting, no shared definitions, data used to explain the past

Stage 2 — Structured

Consistent tracking, basic dashboards, analytics is reactive but reliable

Stage 3 — Proactive ← Most companies are here

Experimentation begins, but A/B tests are ad-hoc and hard to scale

Stage 4 — Systematic

Continuous experimentation, shared hypotheses, feedback loops

Stage 5 — AI-Ready

AI agents can act on trusted, governed, real-time data
